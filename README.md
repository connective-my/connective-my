## Services

### 01 / Web & SaaS Development

Websites, web platforms, and SaaS products designed around the way your business works.

- Responsive websites, e-commerce storefronts, and booking systems
- Product architecture, CMS, and admin dashboards
- Authentication, permissions, subscriptions, and payments
- API integrations, cloud infrastructure, CI/CD, and monitoring

[Web development ↗](https://connective.my/web-development) · [SaaS development ↗](https://connective.my/saas-development)

### 02 / AI Engineering

Intelligent systems that connect models to your business knowledge, applications, and workflows.

- AI assistants, chatbots, and document question answering
- Retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), semantic search, and knowledge bases
- Autonomous agents, multi-agent workflows, and business automation
- Tool, API, and database integrations with human oversight

[AI development ↗](https://connective.my/ai-development) · [RAG development ↗](https://connective.my/rag-development)

The infrastructure around AI agents that helps them operate reliably in production.

- Agent architecture, orchestration, and tool ecosystems
- Guardrails, security, permissions, and error recovery
- Memory, context management, and retrieval systems
- Automated evaluations, observability, tracing, and agent CI/CD

[Explore agent systems ↗](https://connective.my/ai-agents)

Content, editing, and campaign management that gives your brand a consistent presence.

## Our Process

<p align="center">
  <a href="https://connective.my/#process">

1. **Discover** — Understand the problem, users, constraints, requirements, and measures of success.
2. **Design** — Shape the architecture and user experience together, with a clear technical plan.
3. **Build** — Deliver working software in short cycles, with regular progress, testing, and integrations.
4. **Launch** — Deploy, monitor real behaviour, and refine the product as it is used.

## Our Approach

- **Build for dependable use.** Make reliability, error handling, and monitoring part of the work from the start.
- **Keep the scope focused.** Prioritise the capabilities that solve the problem and earn their place.
- **Measure and improve.** Evaluate system behaviour continuously and use the findings to guide the next iteration.
